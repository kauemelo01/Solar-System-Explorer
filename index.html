Sky map detail

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a name to fly to it, double-click to hold it still. Keys: space pause, ← → speed, R reverse, T trails, C hold selected still, Backspace back to the previous selection, / find, F fullscreen.

Planets and Pluto: JPL Keplerian elements (best 1800–2050 AD). Moons: 469 as of Nov 2026; the planets’ moons are fitted to JPL Horizons over 1800–2050 AD (typically within 1.5°), the others use JPL Horizons or published orbits. Rings: NASA fact sheets and occultation studies, radii true, opacity exaggerated. Small bodies: JPL Small-Body Database, placed by orbits fitted to JPL Horizons over 1800–2050 AD. Belts are drawn from typical orbit statistics, not real catalogue entries. Distances and sizes are always true to scale, so most bodies are specks: zoom in, or click a name to fly to it.
