# Moons of dwarf planets and asteroids

**8 moons:** 6 moons of dwarf planets and candidates, and 2 asteroid moons. Pluto's moons have [their own page](moons-pluto.md).

The *Surface image* column says what the app draws on each body; these moons are drawn as plain shapes. Details: [Surface maps](../sources.md#surface-maps). Where JPL Horizons has a satellite solution, the orbit comes from it; otherwise it comes from the peer-reviewed discovery or orbit paper listed. The *a*, *e*, *i* and *P* columns are the values used by the app. The JPL IDs are Horizons SPK IDs.

Moons whose orbit could not be found in a verifiable published source are not included (a [limitation of the model](../sources.md#limitations-of-the-model); see [What's not included](../sources.md#whats-not-included)); this is why Makemake's moon (S/2015 (136472) 1, "MK2", whose orbit is only bounded) and Ida's moon Dactyl are absent.

## Notes

- **Frames.** Horizons elements are J2000 ecliptic, relative to the primary. Hiʻiaka and Namaka are J2000 ecliptic as published. Xiangliu's published orbit has a mirror ambiguity (prograde or retrograde); the app uses the prograde solution, with angles taken in the J2000 equatorial frame of the published astrometry, so its orientation is uncertain (a [limitation of the model](../sources.md#limitations-of-the-model)).
- **Selam** orbits Dinkinesh at about 3.1 km with a period of about 52.7 h and is tidally locked. Its orbit plane is not published in a source we could verify, so the app places it in the ecliptic plane; only its distance and period are measured (a [limitation of the model](../sources.md#limitations-of-the-model)).
- **Sizes.** Dysnomia, Weywot and Vanth radii are from their JPL Horizons records. Hiʻiaka (≈ 320 km) and Namaka (≈ 160 km) diameters are from Ragozzine & Brown (2009) as compiled by Johnston's Archive. Dimorphos is 160 m across ([NASA DART](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/dart/)). Each lobe of Selam is about 220 m ([NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/asteroids/dinkinesh/)). Xiangliu is smaller than 100 km (Kiss et al. 2019); the app uses a placeholder, marked *est.* (a [limitation of the model](../sources.md#limitations-of-the-model))
- **In the app.** The moons menu lists them under their parent: *Dwarf planets & candidates* (Eris, Haumea, then the candidates Quaoar, Orcus and Gonggong, after Pluto) and *Asteroids* (Didymos, Dinkinesh), with All / None for each group. A moon is shown when it is switched on and its parent's group is shown (*Dwarf planets*, *Candidates* or *Asteroids & centaurs*); switching a moon on also switches its parent's group on.
