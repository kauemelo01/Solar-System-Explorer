# Moons of Pluto

**5 moons.** Orbits use the **JPL mean orbital elements** of planetary satellites (Laplace-plane or ecliptic elements as published by JPL SSD). The *a*, *e*, *i* and *P* columns below are the exact values used by the app. Every moon links to its **JPL Horizons** object record by NAIF ID, and to its NASA Science page where NASA has published one.

- NASA Science overview: <https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/pluto/moons/>
- JPL mean elements table: <https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/elem/sep.html>
- JPL physical parameters: <https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/phys_par/>
- JPL discovery circumstances: <https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/discovery.html>
- IAU Minor Planet Center satellite announcements (MPECs): <https://minorplanetcenter.net/mpec/RecentMPECs.html>

Radius values marked *est.* are not published; the app uses a placeholder size for display only.
